# Touhou University Research Pack

Research date: 2026-07-24  
Project: `touhou_university_website`  
Status: working editorial baseline for an unofficial, non-commercial fan website

## Purpose

This folder records the evidence and editorial decisions behind the fictional
“Touhou University” website. The site is an alternate-universe institutional
translation of Gensokyo: canon identities and social structures are retained,
while the university, its admissions process, faculties, statistics, projects
and campus history are explicitly project-original.

The research follows the method already established in
`/home/pentester/Project/gensokyo-monochrome-heart/design/11_research/research_method.md`:

1. official profiles and works anchor factual claims;
2. official-adjacent translations help locate passages;
3. community references are navigation aids, not the sole basis for disputed
   character claims;
4. fanon is labelled and never silently promoted to canon;
5. in-universe narrators such as Aya and Akyuu are treated as biased witnesses.

## Files

- `source-register.md` — source hierarchy, links, and legal/fan-work review.
- `world-and-institution-notes.md` — what Gensokyo already provides for an
  educational institution and what the AU adds.
- `faculty-matrix.md` — character-to-faculty mapping with canon anchors and
  adaptation guardrails.
- `content-and-provenance.md` — site content decisions and asset ledger.
- `academic-catalogue-and-transit.md` — programme credits and fees, route-graph
  assumptions, and the spell-card research design.
- `spellcard-workshop-notes.md` — six independent reviewer positions,
  lightweight sandbox constraints, public-defence structure and persistence.
- `phantasm-campus-notes.md` — counterfactual-campus concept, six hard unlock
  seals, ordinary-site clue budget, dream curriculum and ledger isolation.

## Internal editorial labels

- **原作錨點 / Canon anchor** — a concise identity, ability, role or relationship
  supported by official works.
- **校方設定 / University AU** — an invention created specifically for this site.
- **傳聞 / In-world claim** — an account attributed to a fallible Gensokyo
  narrator or publication.

These labels belong only in working research and are not shown inside ordinary
public-facing university content. The site does not reproduce game scripts,
endings, sprites, screenshots, music or scans.
