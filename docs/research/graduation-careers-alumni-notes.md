# Graduation, Careers and Hyakki Yagyo Alumni Notes

Research and implementation date: 2026-07-28
Public route: `careers.html`

## Institutional premise

Graduation is an audit of evidence, not a cheerful checklist. Eight desks read
eight different parts of the same local student life:

1. registered credits and each school’s five core courses;
2. graded coursework and a methods examination;
3. a thesis or spell-card public defence;
4. a live research-ethics file;
5. at least one returned fieldwork placement;
6. active library holdings and any objection by the object itself;
7. residence checkout and open room-transfer business;
8. incident, property and chronology disputes.

Missing evidence blocks a degree. Conditional and contested evidence may
travel with the degree only when the student explicitly accepts the unabridged
annex. This keeps the institution fallible without making its files
meaningless. PHANTASM results never enter the official credit total; an
existing dream transcript may instead show as a plus-or-minus-three trace on
the reverse of the printed degree.

The four time/lifespan tracks preserve useful Gensokyo problems: an ordinary
four-year record, a long-lived forty-year record, a fairy repeat record for a
student who repeatedly forgets graduating, and a chronology dispute where an
enrolment date may follow graduation. The audit never silently repairs the
last case.

## Careers Office

The office publishes twelve real destination files rather than generic
“industry” labels:

- Hakurei boundary care and donation-box custody;
- Scarlet Devil Mansion zero-minute archives;
- Eientei night handover;
- kappa public repair;
- Bunbunmaru corrections;
- Terakoya missing-year teaching;
- Myouren common-table work;
- Moriya wind infrastructure;
- Hakugyokurou phantom logistics;
- Chireiden private-evidence handling;
- Kourindou purpose and naming;
- Sanzu queue appeals.

Every file states actual duties, compensation, duty bell, commute, host,
friction, an uncrossable boundary and an interview question. Matching is
transparent: school, desired work domains, schedule, travel, compensation,
trouble tolerance and existing on-device evidence contribute named reasons or
cautions. The result ranks all twelve paths and stores the five strongest,
but “likely to become an incident first” remains a valid, visible band rather
than being discarded. Referrals require a note and are sent once per opening
through the shared causal ledger.

## Hyakki Yagyo Alumni

The public association is browsable by everyone; a personal alumni file
requires an unsealed degree. Its eight chapters are organised by unresolved
questions and routes—Village, Misty Lake, mountain, Eientei, underground,
Higan, Hakugyokurou and Outside drift—not by species, lifespan or whether the
member is alive.

Date, lunar phase and duty bell rotate the current lantern route and invitation
version. Alumni may RSVP, keep a nameplate while absent, and volunteer as a
returning fieldwork mentor while declaring what they will not do for the
student. A profile activated before its degree time remains as a chronology
variant rather than being normalised away.

## Data and causal boundaries

Five portable on-device files are registered:

- `tu:graduation:audits`
- `tu:graduation:degrees`
- `tu:careers:draft`
- `tu:careers:plans`
- `tu:alumni:profile`

Seven schema-2 events form three causal chains:

- audit requested → degree issued → alumni profile activated → reunion RSVP or
  mentorship offered;
- career plan submitted → referral sent.

The public search/domain registry projects jobs, chapters and the visitor’s
own records without making another copy. My TU shows a combined count. Campus
BBS receives derived notices. Hieda dossier 12 binds the graduation capstone,
Terakoya opening, Higan chapter, early admission letter, Hakugyokurou field
seal and exact first-parent chronicle record.

## Editorial guardrails

- A degree proves the audit occurred; it does not declare every dispute solved.
- Long-lived, fairy and chronology cases remain playable records rather than
  jokes detached from the rules.
- Career chaos comes from concrete character and institutional friction, not
  random nonsense.
- Public copy stays in-world. Fan-work and source status remain in the global
  notices and internal research files.
- Chinese, Japanese and English are authored for every public destination,
  chapter, track and interaction.
