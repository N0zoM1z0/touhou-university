# Gensokyo Research Ethics Review Board

Research/design date: 2026-07-26
Public surface: `ethics.html`

## Editorial purpose

The board should create friction between abilities, persons, objects and an
institution that cannot make every contradiction tidy. It is not a generic
modern compliance form placed over Gensokyo. Each reviewer brings a recognisable
problem, sometimes making the file harder to close:

- Eirin asks about dose, risk, observable harm and who can stop first.
- Satori treats reading without writing as information processing and separates
  consent to participate from consent to mental access.
- Akyuu and Keine ask what a record, correction, deletion receipt and right to
  be forgotten can mean when history itself is editable.
- Eiki asks who carries responsibility, whether refusal changes access to other
  services, and whether appeal returns to the same interested party.
- Reimu asks whether the rule can be explained on one notice and enforced when
  time, wavelength or history has already been altered.

Akyuu binds the five opinions into one file, but does not average them. A
single blocking concern can require revision; a conflict that cannot honestly
be collapsed remains contested and keeps its minority opinion.

## Five opening disputes

1. Reisen changes wavelength during an orientation study without prior notice.
   The research need for blinding does not itself grant permission to alter or
   read a participant.
2. Satori reads thoughts but says she records nothing. The design treats a
   changed question, inference or later decision as a consequence even when no
   quotation reaches paper.
3. Sakuya places a control group inside stopped time. “Zero outside minutes”
   is not a usable exposure dose, and an independent witness cannot depend on
   the same stopped clock.
4. Keine deletes interview history as fulfilment of data deletion. Complete
   erasure conflicts with retaining a content-free receipt proving that a
   withdrawal request was honoured.
5. Drift Reader 77 says not to open its back panel while Kourindou holds the
   salvage slip. Custody and object assent answer different questions; either
   holder or potentially willing object may force a non-invasive method.

These are opening arguments rather than canonical answers. A visitor can alter
the safeguards and discover which seat changes its own opinion.

## Ruling and lifecycle model

The public outcomes are approved, approved with conditions, revision required,
contested and withdrawn. They are determined by the strongest unresolved kind
of objection, never a numeric score:

1. `revise` blocks the start until an identified repair is submitted;
2. `contested` preserves an irreducible rights or record dispute;
3. `conditional` allows work only with that seat's conditions;
4. `approved` requires every seat to have no unresolved stronger stance.

Formal submission writes one protocol and one five-opinion review. Amendment
creates a new protocol version and marks the previous one superseded without
changing its contents. Withdrawal adds a reason and event to the selected
version but retains the review and version chain.

The three on-device files are:

- `tu:ethics:drafts`
- `tu:ethics:protocols`
- `tu:ethics:reviews`

The four official lifecycle events are:

- `ethics.protocol.submitted`
- `ethics.review.completed`
- `ethics.protocol.amended`
- `ethics.protocol.withdrawn`

Search, My TU, BBS, incident files and the Hieda index read those sources or
their shared event projection. Derived BBS posts are not visitor-authored
`tu:bbs:posts`, and the Hieda graph never becomes a second source of truth.

## Interaction and tone

The visual language is a repaired paper review desk: separate seat slips,
misaligned red stamps, marginal voices and version binding. It should remain
readable and keyboard-operable instead of simulating illegible bureaucracy.
The five exact case routes and every saved protocol route must be shareable,
returnable with browser Back, printable through the shared print desk and
usable in Traditional Chinese, Japanese and English.

Public copy stays inside the university fiction. These internal notes may
discuss editorial construction; the public board should simply behave as an
institution that exists in Gensokyo.
