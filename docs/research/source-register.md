# Source Register and Rights Review

Last checked: 2026-07-27

## 1. Rights and fan-work presentation

Primary policy:

- [東方Projectの二次創作ガイドライン](https://touhou-project.news/guideline/)
  (updated 2024-05-31)
- [Guidelines for Touhou Project Fan Creators](https://touhou-project.news/guidelines_en/)
  (English page, updated 2020-11-10)

Operational conclusions for this website:

- identify the site clearly as an unofficial Touhou Project fan work;
- do not suggest that Team Shanghai Alice or ZUN created, sponsors, endorses or
  operates the fictional university;
- do not use extracted game assets, endings, other fan creators' work, or copied
  official text;
- use original implementation, original generated campus illustrations and
  short factual summaries;
- keep the public repository non-commercial and recheck the official guideline
  if hosting, monetisation or distribution changes.

This is an editorial compliance record, not legal advice.

## 5. Claims deliberately excluded

- no definitive character ages where canon is ambiguous;
- no canon romantic pairings;
- no claim that the fictional university exists in official continuity;
- no meme traits as job qualifications;
- no “power ranking” presented as scholarship;
- no copied game dialogue or quoted endings;
- no official-looking accreditation, trademark registration or endorsement.
