EX-L011. What minimum information must be stated to make the incident traceable without vaguely calling every problem “an old version”?12 marks

Source dossier · independent conditions
[Route notice history]
v4.2 05:50: “At full moon, turn right after lantern four.”
v4.2-k 06:02 (kappa-terminal cache): “At full moon, go straight after lantern four.”
v4.3 06:11: “At full moon, turn right after lantern three; lantern four is a decoy.”
[Distribution] Central board received v4.3 at 06:12; rabbit station 06:18; kappa terminal still showed v4.2-k until 07:04.
[Incident] At 06:37 a visitor followed the terminal straight and returned to Yesterday's Meeting Point.
  1. The terminal viewed, displayed version v4.2-k, access time 06:37, and the exact divergence of v4.2-k from official v4.2/v4.3
  2. Only say “the kappa equipment was old”
  3. Record only the correct v4.3 text
  4. Delete v4.2-k so the version history looks consistent
Answer: A

ExplanationThis is not mere lag: the cached variant altered the action. Device, version, time, and diff are all needed to separate bad generation from delayed distribution.

EX-L022. Which interpretation survives clock correction without exceeding the data?12 marks

Source dossier · independent conditions
[Testimony] Reisen: “I closed the moonlight gate after the second bell.” Tewi: “The gate dimmed first, then the second bell sounded.”
[Devices] Bell recorder clock is 18 seconds slow; gate controller resets to 00:00 after each full-moon switch; corridor light meter is correctly timed.
[Illuminance] 80→12 at 21:14:42; recorder labels the second bell 21:14:31.
  1. Conflicting testimony invalidates every instrument record
  2. Adding 18 seconds places bell two near 21:14:49, after the light drop; Tewi's observed order is compatible, but this does not establish that Reisen lied
  3. The 21:14:31 label proves the bell came first
  4. The gate-controller reset also reset the light meter
Answer: B

ExplanationCorrect the known recorder offset first. Action/perception lag remains, so event order—not honesty—is identifiable.

EX-M011. At full moon, what route reaches E earliest and when?14 marks

Source dossier · independent conditions
From 08:00: G–B=8; B–E=12 (entry only during minutes 10–19 each hour); G–W=5; W–E=9 (at full moon, entry closed on odd-numbered minutes); G–E=30. Waiting is allowed. Depart G at 08:07:30; round arrivals up to a whole minute before testing entry.
  1. G–W–E at 08:22
  2. G–E at 08:38
  3. G–B–E at 08:28
  4. G–W–E at 08:23
Answer: D

ExplanationReach W at 08:12:30; rounding gives 08:13, an odd minute when entry is closed. Enter at 08:14 and travel 9 minutes, so G–W–E arrives at 08:23. G–B–E reaches B at 08:15:30, rounds to an admissible 08:16, and arrives at 08:28.
