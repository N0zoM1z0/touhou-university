EX-L011. What minimum information must be stated to make the incident traceable without vaguely calling every problem “an old version”?12 marks

Source dossier · independent conditions
[Route notice history]

v4.2-k 06:02 (kappa-terminal cache): “At full moon, go straight after lantern four.”
v4.3 06:11: “At full moon, turn right after lantern three; lantern four is a decoy.”

[Incident] At 06:37 a visitor followed the terminal straight and returned to Yesterday's Meeting Point.
  1. The terminal viewed, displayed version v4.2-k, access time 06:37, and the exact divergence of v4.2-k from official v4.2/v4.3
  2. Record the kappa terminal, 06:37 access, and missing v4.3; classify ordinary distribution lag without preserving the exact displayed text
  3. Record v4.2-k and the visitor's straight route; compare only with v4.3 and treat every difference as update lag

ExplanationThis is not mere lag: the cached variant altered the action. Device, version, time, and diff are all needed to separate bad generation from delayed distribution.

EX-L022. Which interpretation survives clock correction without exceeding the data?12 marks

Source dossier · independent conditions
[Testimony] Reisen: “I closed the moonlight gate after the second bell.” Tewi: “The gate dimmed first, then the second bell sounded.”
[Devices] Bell recorder clock is 18 seconds slow; gate controller resets to 00:00 after each full-moon switch; corridor light meter is correctly timed.
[Illuminance] 80→12 at 21:14:42; recorder labels the second bell 21:14:31.
  1. The light meter times visible dimming only; with the controller reset, the action is untimed, so testimony should be ranked by the speakers' institutional roles
  2. After correction the bell is about 21:14:49 and dimming came first; therefore Tewi's account is true and Reisen falsely reported the closing time
  3. Adding 18 seconds places bell two near 21:14:49, after the light drop; Tewi's observed order is compatible, but this does not establish that Reisen lied
  4. Because the recorder is 18 seconds slow, subtract 18 seconds to get 21:14:13; the bell precedes the light drop and supports Reisen's order
Answer: C

ExplanationCorrect the known recorder offset first. Action/perception lag remains, so event order—not honesty—is identifiable.

[Catalogue] Copy B used a kappa auto-segmenter known to join the characters around “lake; gatekeeper.”

  1. The segmentation fault shows possible machine bias only; once disclosed, B can still join the original and A with equal weight in a semantic majority vote
  2. The original and Copy A make the gatekeeper the subject, but mist may have acquired guarding ability; the objection is only that ability evidence is missing
  3. B presents a different syntax that may preserve another oral version; count the original, A, and B as one vote each and seek more copies
  4. The claim depends on a known segmentation failure and conflicts with original punctuation plus independent Copy A; B is not independent semantic evidence

ExplanationThe key is provenance: B is not another witness but a derivative reading produced by a known tool failure.
