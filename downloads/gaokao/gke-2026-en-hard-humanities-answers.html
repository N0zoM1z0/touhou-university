HD-L011. Which description best states the error without turning an unproved motive into fact?12 marks

Source dossier · independent conditions
[Paper 08:10] North Windway reopened.
[Workshop note 08:06] Third support held with kappa tape; one-person load, awaiting reinspection.
[Correction 08:32] “Reopened” removed; “inspection staff only” substituted.
  1. The one-person note proves the tape cannot support any ordinary student passage
  2. The 08:10 reporter deliberately hid a support failure already confirmed by the workshop
  3. The 08:10 headline expanded a restricted inspection state into general access
  4. The 08:32 correction introduced the restriction; the workshop had reported no load limit before then
Answer: C

ExplanationThe workshop note predates the report. The evidence proves overgeneralised access, not motive or tape lifetime.

HD-L022. Which rewrite is the most complete actionable instruction for that day?12 marks

[Reverse-side addition] After rain the tracks point backwards; use the medicinal scent.
Daily log: rain last night, calm at 09:00, medicinal scent from southeast at 09:07.
  1. After the bell, do not follow the track direction; in calm air proceed southeast toward the scent source
  2. After the bell, follow the tracks and reverse only if the scent disappears in the southeast
  3. After the bell, walk opposite the tracks because the reverse-side note supersedes scent guidance

HD-L033. Which research summary best preserves A's rhetoric while keeping evidence levels distinct?12 marks

Source dossier · independent conditions
A: “The gate did not move; the road forgot it.”
B: “At dragon hour the gateposts remained two lengths north of the old boundary stone; at snake hour the road ended in the bamboo forest.”
C: “Everyone knows Yukari was cheerful this morning.”
  1. “The road forgot the gate” means Yukari's mood altered the road, while the gatepost measurement shows she did not move the gate
  2. Because the posts were fixed at dragon hour, A proves the road connected to bamboo only at snake hour and necessarily later returned
  3. A is rhetorical and B measured, so their different evidence types cannot be compared in one summary
  4. “The road forgot the gate” can operationally mean that the gateposts stayed fixed while the road connection changed between the two hours; cause remains undetermined
Answer: D

ExplanationA sound summary maps local rhetoric to observable variables while leaving causation open.
