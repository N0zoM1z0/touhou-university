HD-L011. Which description best states the error without turning an unproved motive into fact?12 marks

Source dossier · independent conditions
[Paper 08:10] North Windway reopened.
[Workshop note 08:06] Third support held with kappa tape; one-person load, awaiting reinspection.
[Correction 08:32] “Reopened” removed; “inspection staff only” substituted.
  1. The 08:10 headline expanded a restricted inspection state into general access
  2. The workshop did not report until 08:32
  3. Kappa tape necessarily fails within fifteen minutes
  4. The reporter deliberately concealed the support hazard
Answer: A

ExplanationThe workshop note predates the report. The evidence proves overgeneralised access, not motive or tape lifetime.

[Reverse-side addition] After rain the tracks point backwards; use the medicinal scent.

  1. Wait for the next rain to erase the tracks

HD-L033. Which research summary best preserves A's rhetoric while keeping evidence levels distinct?12 marks

Source dossier · independent conditions
A: “The gate did not move; the road forgot it.”
B: “At dragon hour the gateposts remained two lengths north of the old boundary stone; at snake hour the road ended in the bamboo forest.”
C: “Everyone knows Yukari was cheerful this morning.”
  1. Neither gate nor road changed
  2. Because A is figurative, all measurements in B are invalid
  3. “The road forgot the gate” can operationally mean that the gateposts stayed fixed while the road connection changed between the two hours; cause remains undetermined
  4. Yukari was cheerful, so the road necessarily obeyed her
Answer: C

ExplanationA sound summary maps local rhetoric to observable variables while leaving causation open.
