01 / GKL-01

Gensokyo Language & Letters

Reading, source expression, notices, and news rhetoric

36 marks

L011. The paper first reports “North windway permanently repaired,” then corrects it to “kappa tape still counts as temporary.” What does the correction chiefly restore?6 marks

  1. The repair status and its standard
  2. The windway altitude
  3. The reporter's flight speed
  4. The sentence's rhyme
Answer: A

ExplanationThe changed fact is whether the repair is permanent or temporary, which affects travel and risk decisions.

L022. A bamboo notice says: “Do not stop at the second identical meeting point; it is last week's.” What is the soundest reading?6 marks

  1. No meeting point can be used
  2. Temporal displacement is part of route identification
  3. The notice orders a return
  4. The second point is always nearer
Answer: B

ExplanationThe notice combines spatial and temporal evidence; identical appearance does not prove identical place.

L033. Which sentence is best suited to a research field record?6 marks

  1. Yesterday was supposedly worse
  2. Everyone knows fairies are unreliable
  3. After the third bell, two of five eastern lamps went out
  4. This is definitely another incident
Answer: C

ExplanationIt records time, direction, total count, and change, allowing later verification.

L044. For the same minor incident, which headline least presents conjecture as fact?6 marks

  1. Kappa Admit Time Has Ended
  2. History's Slowest Minute Arrives
  3. Youkai Mountain Deliberately Stops Every Campus Clock
  4. Campus Clocks Stop; Cause Under Investigation
Answer: D

ExplanationThe stoppage is observed, while “under investigation” clearly marks the cause as unknown.

L055. A source reads, “The gate remained but the path was lost; visitors followed the bell.” What does “lost” chiefly mean here?6 marks

  1. Vanished and could no longer be followed
  2. Was borrowed
  3. Died
  4. Fled
Answer: A

ExplanationThe contrast is between a remaining gate and an untraceable path, so the sense is disappearance.

L066. Which sequence makes a mistaken campus alert into the most traceable correction? ① impact scope ② preserve original headline ③ identify error ④ give correct information and time6 marks

  1. ④ only
  2. ②③④①
  3. ④②①③
  4. ③①②④
Answer: B

ExplanationPreserve the original, identify the error, supply corrected dated information, then state the affected scope.

02 / GKM-02

Boundary Mathematics

Ratios, route networks, lunar cycles, and danmaku geometry

42 marks

M011. Using a 29.5-day lunar cycle, about how many complete cycles are 59 days?7 marks

  1. 1
  2. 1.5
  3. 2
  4. 2.5
Answer: C

Explanation59 ÷ 29.5 = 2.

M022. A turbine rises from 80 to 100 output units per minute. What is the percentage increase?7 marks

  1. 40%
  2. 80%
  3. 20%
  4. 25%
Answer: D

ExplanationThe increase is 20; 20 ÷ 80 = 25%.

M033. Gate to workshop takes 31 minutes walking. Windway: walk 6, wait 4, ride 12, walk 5. How many minutes are saved?7 marks

  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 8
Answer: A

ExplanationThe windway total is 27 minutes, saving 31 − 27 = 4.

M044. Three equally spaced bullets are placed in each of eight directions, excluding the centre. How many bullets?7 marks

  1. 16
  2. 24
  3. 27
  4. 11
Answer: B

Explanation8 directions × 3 bullets = 24.

M055. A prescription is 0.15 units per kilogram for a 48 kg participant. What is the total dose?7 marks

  1. 32
  2. 6.2
  3. 7.2
  4. 8.0
Answer: C

Explanation0.15 × 48 = 7.2; the unit should remain attached in formal work.

M066. A barrier reflection maps (3, −2) to (−3, −2). Across which line is it reflected?7 marks

  1. y=x
  2. x=−2
  3. x-axis
  4. y-axis
Answer: D

ExplanationOnly x changes sign, so the reflection is across the y-axis.

03 / GKC-03

Shared Gensokyo Knowledge

Rules, campus judgment, incidents, and shared life

30 marks

C011. What is most indispensable before a spell-card contest begins?5 marks

  1. A name, rules, and a legible exit
  2. Anonymous spectators
  3. A newspaper extra
  4. Greater firepower
Answer: A

ExplanationThese elements make the contest identifiable and endable; raw power does not create rules.

C022. A library book starts flying toward an open window. What is the best first step?5 marks

  1. Open more windows
  2. Note the call number, alert staff, and close nearby windows
  3. Apply an unknown seal immediately
  4. Declare it a free-range collection
Answer: B

ExplanationControl the reversible environmental risk, preserve identification, and involve the responsible staff.

C033. A kappa prototype sparks blue while its note says normal sparks are green. What should you do?5 marks

  1. Edit the note to say blue
  2. Assume a new feature and continue
  3. Stop, preserve the scene, and call workshop duty staff
  4. Cover the sparks with kappa tape
Answer: C

ExplanationThe colour differs from the documented normal state; stop before rewriting the standard.

C044. A misleading headline was written by the corrections lecturer. What is the soundest response?5 marks

  1. Admit it only in class
  2. Delete the whole term's paper
  3. Faculty status waives correction
  4. Publish a correction and record the conflict in the case
Answer: D

ExplanationHer institutional role increases the need for a visible correction and conflict disclosure; it does not waive it.

C055. On a full-moon clinic visit, the main corridor is closed. Which route should be used?5 marks

  1. Main House—Pharmacy—Clinic
  2. Yesterday's Clearing—campus bell
  3. Follow the fourth lantern
  4. Main House straight to Clinic
Answer: A

ExplanationFull-moon flow diverts through Pharmacy; a fourth lantern is not reliable guidance.

C066. Yukari moves a classroom “between presence and absence.” What should a student verify first?5 marks

  1. Aya's headline length
  2. Whether policy defines only on-campus addresses
  3. Marisa's materials box
  4. Today's dessert
Answer: B

ExplanationThe issue turns on institutional definitions of location and attendance; inspect those boundaries first.

04 / GKH-04

Humanities Comprehensive

History, geography, journalism, faith, and public life

42 marks

H011. Three sources disagree on an incident date. What is the best historical method?7 marks

  1. Delete the latest source
  2. Choose the best-written source
  3. Compare date of composition, author position, and calendars used
  4. Average the dates
Answer: C

ExplanationThe disagreement is evidence; source conditions and calendars can explain how it arose.

H022. Shrine, temple, and hermitage contest one public-event budget. Which plan best demonstrates public negotiation?7 marks

  1. Give it to the loudest
  2. Make the table smaller
  3. Split equally and forbid further debate
  4. Publish aims, non-negotiables, shared infrastructure costs, and dissent
Answer: D

ExplanationNegotiation makes resources, positions, and unresolved dissent visible instead of pretending conflict vanished.

H033. The Bamboo Forest shifts daily while Eientei's gate is relatively stable. How should a map represent this?7 marks

  1. Distinguish fixed anchors, conditional routes, and update time
  2. Draw only the prettiest bamboo
  3. Simply remove scale
  4. Draw every line as permanent
Answer: A

ExplanationConditional geography must communicate stability and freshness, not disguise temporary routes as fixed.

H044. Keine distinguishes what happened, what was recorded, and what was permitted to remain remembered. Which relation is closest?7 marks

  1. They are necessarily identical
  2. Records and memory can be shaped by power, medium, and loss
  3. Only the event is unimportant
  4. Being remembered makes it true
Answer: B

ExplanationHistorical method studies selections and losses between event, record, and public memory.

H055. Aya publishes the headline first and verifies later. What is the largest institutional risk?7 marks

  1. Readers become faster
  2. The headline is too short
  3. The error shapes action and reputation before correction
  4. Paper use falls
Answer: C

ExplanationA later correction cannot automatically reverse evacuations, accusations, or resource decisions already made.

H066. The Hakurei donation box repeatedly leaves campus. Which research question is most analytical?7 marks

  1. Is red faster?
  2. Who draws the best arrows?
  3. Is the box capricious?
  4. How ownership, purpose, and location rules conflict between shrine and university
Answer: D

ExplanationIt locates the recurring event in competing institutional claims instead of ending with personification.
