01 / GKL-01

Gensokyo Language & Letters

Reading, source expression, notices, and news rhetoric

36 marks

L011. The paper first reports “North windway permanently repaired,” then corrects it to “kappa tape still counts as temporary.” What does the correction chiefly restore?6 marks

  1. The publication sequence, without correcting the permanent–temporary distinction
  2. A more cautious tone for “permanent,” without changing the access judgment
  3. The repair status and the standard used to classify it
  4. The tape's material name, without stating whether passage is currently allowed
Answer: C

ExplanationThe changed fact is whether the repair is permanent or temporary, which affects travel and risk decisions.

L022. A bamboo notice says: “Do not stop at the second identical meeting point; it is last week's.” What is the soundest reading?6 marks

  1. Route identification must check both spatial position and associated time
  2. “Second” means the traveller overshot and should return to the first point
  3. “Last week's” dates the notice and does not affect the point now in view
  4. Because they look identical, treat the later one as a reserve meeting point
Answer: A

ExplanationThe notice combines spatial and temporal evidence; identical appearance does not prove identical place.

L033. Which sentence is best suited to a research field record?6 marks

  1. The third bell caused a minor incident and the lighting failure
  2. A duty worker says more failed yesterday, so today's condition should count as normal
  3. Fairies probably extinguished the eastern lamps, because they are usually unreliable
  4. After the third bell, two of five eastern lamps went out
Answer: D

ExplanationIt records time, direction, total count, and change, allowing later verification.

L044. For the same minor incident, which headline least presents conjecture as fact?6 marks

  1. Mountain works stop campus clocks; university investigates responsibility
  2. Campus clocks stop; cause remains under investigation
  3. Kappa deny changing time settings, so the university rules out equipment failure
  4. The stoppage coincides with a Mountain trial, making the works the sole cause
Answer: B

ExplanationThe stoppage is observed, while “under investigation” clearly marks the cause as unknown.

L055. A source reads, “The gate remained but the path was lost; visitors followed the bell.” What does “lost” chiefly mean here?6 marks

  1. The road vanished and could no longer be followed
  2. The road was lent elsewhere, requiring visitors to wait for its return
  3. The road was destroyed, forcing visitors to navigate by the bell
  4. The road fled its former place but could still be followed by its traces
Answer: A

ExplanationThe contrast is between a remaining gate and an untraceable path, so the sense is disappearance.

L066. Which sequence makes a mistaken campus alert into the most traceable correction? ① impact scope ② preserve original headline ③ identify error ④ give correct information and time6 marks

  1. ③①②④: name the error and scope before preserving and correcting
  2. ④①③②: give the update and scope now, preserving the original only afterward
  3. ②③④①: preserve, identify, give the timed correction, then state scope
  4. ④②①③: publish the new information first and identify the error last
Answer: C

ExplanationPreserve the original, identify the error, supply corrected dated information, then state the affected scope.

02 / GKM-02

Boundary Mathematics

Ratios, route networks, lunar cycles, and danmaku geometry

42 marks

M011. Using a 29.5-day lunar cycle, about how many complete cycles are 59 days?7 marks

  1. 1.5
  2. 2
  3. 2.5
  4. 1
Answer: B

Explanation59 ÷ 29.5 = 2.

M022. A turbine rises from 80 to 100 output units per minute. What is the percentage increase?7 marks

  1. 40%
  2. 80%
  3. 20%
  4. 25%
Answer: D

ExplanationThe increase is 20; 20 ÷ 80 = 25%.

M033. Gate to workshop takes 31 minutes walking. Windway: walk 6, wait 4, ride 12, walk 5. How many minutes are saved?7 marks

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 4
  4. 5
Answer: C

ExplanationThe windway total is 27 minutes, saving 31 − 27 = 4.

M044. Three equally spaced bullets are placed in each of eight directions, excluding the centre. How many bullets?7 marks

  1. 24
  2. 27
  3. 11
  4. 16
Answer: A

Explanation8 directions × 3 bullets = 24.

M055. A prescription is 0.15 units per kilogram for a 48 kg participant. What is the total dose?7 marks

  1. 8.0
  2. 32
  3. 6.2
  4. 7.2
Answer: D

Explanation0.15 × 48 = 7.2; the unit should remain attached in formal work.

M066. A barrier reflection maps (3, −2) to (−3, −2). Across which line is it reflected?7 marks

  1. x-axis
  2. y-axis
  3. y=x
  4. x=−2
Answer: B

ExplanationOnly x changes sign, so the reflection is across the y-axis.

03 / GKC-03

Shared Gensokyo Knowledge

Rules, campus judgment, incidents, and shared life

30 marks

C011. What is most indispensable before a spell-card contest begins?5 marks

  1. Let the referee hold the full rules while participants know only the start signal
  2. Set the name and exit first, then write the rules around the opening round
  3. Publish the name and round count, leaving termination to the side with greater firepower
  4. Have both sides confirm the name, rules, and an exit each can identify
Answer: D

ExplanationThese elements make the contest identifiable and endable; raw power does not create rules.

C022. A library book starts flying toward an open window. What is the best first step?5 marks

  1. Close the window first and identify the book later from memory of its cover
  2. Note the call number, alert staff, and close nearby windows
  3. Restrain it with the nearest collection seal, then ask whether that seal was compatible
  4. Move it into an empty display case for observation without yet alerting staff or readers
Answer: B

ExplanationControl the reversible environmental risk, preserve identification, and involve the responsible staff.

C033. A kappa prototype sparks blue while its note says normal sparks are green. What should you do?5 marks

  1. Stop operation, preserve the scene, and notify workshop duty staff
  2. Remove power and casing immediately, documenting the original state afterward
  3. Quarantine the machine and revise the normal colour to blue pending the next shift
  4. Record the blue sparks and continue at low power to see whether they are a new feature
Answer: A

ExplanationThe colour differs from the documented normal state; stop before rewriting the standard.

C044. A misleading headline was written by the corrections lecturer. What is the soundest response?5 marks

  1. Retain the headline and disclose the author's role, leaving credibility to readers
  2. Correct the facts publicly but omit the author's role to avoid personalizing the issue
  3. Publish a correction and record the author's conflict of interest in the case
  4. Wait for internal review, then silently replace the original headline
Answer: C

ExplanationHer institutional role increases the need for a visible correction and conflict disclosure; it does not waive it.

C055. On a full-moon clinic visit, the main corridor is closed. Which route should be used?5 marks

  1. Take the Main House corridor because the clinic remains in the same complex
  2. Follow full-moon diversion from Main House through Pharmacy to the Clinic
  3. Wait at Yesterday's Clearing and infer the clinic direction from the campus bell
  4. Bypass the corridor via the fourth lantern, treating night signals as fresher than the map
Answer: B

ExplanationFull-moon flow diverts through Pharmacy; a fourth lantern is not reliable guidance.

C066. Yukari moves a classroom “between presence and absence.” What should a student verify first?5 marks

  1. Which side the teacher names as the room, using that declaration in place of policy
  2. Whether the campus router currently classifies the signal as on or off campus
  3. Whether the roll marks the student absent, then use that result to define the location
  4. Whether policy definitions of class location and attendance cover such an address
Answer: D

ExplanationThe issue turns on institutional definitions of location and attendance; inspect those boundaries first.

04 / GKH-04

Humanities Comprehensive

History, geography, journalism, faith, and public life

42 marks

H011. Three sources disagree on an incident date. What is the best historical method?7 marks

  1. Exclude the latest source until its date can be made to agree with the other two
  2. Convert all dates to one calendar, then average them into an incident date
  3. Compare composition date, author position, and the calendar each source used
  4. Prefer the earliest composition because it is less exposed to later memory
Answer: C

ExplanationThe disagreement is evidence; source conditions and calendars can explain how it arose.

H022. Shrine, temple, and hermitage contest one public-event budget. Which plan best demonstrates public negotiation?7 marks

  1. Publish each party's aims, non-negotiables, shared costs, and remaining dissent
  2. Weight funding by forecast attendance and publish only the adopted plan
  3. Draw lots among complete proposals and deny shared facilities to the losing parties
  4. Split the budget equally first and require each party to fit its aims to that share
Answer: A

ExplanationNegotiation makes resources, positions, and unresolved dissent visible instead of pretending conflict vanished.

H033. The Bamboo Forest shifts daily while Eientei's gate is relatively stable. How should a map represent this?7 marks

  1. Distinguish fixed anchors from conditional routes and timestamp each layer
  2. Print only each day's routes so fixed anchors do not create false confidence
  3. Keep scale and gate, placing all route uncertainty in a note below the map
  4. Draw the latest surveyed paths as solid lines and use the gate only for orientation
Answer: A

ExplanationConditional geography must communicate stability and freshness, not disguise temporary routes as fixed.

H044. Keine distinguishes what happened, what was recorded, and what was permitted to remain remembered. Which relation is closest?7 marks

  1. Long-corrected public memory is more reliable than contemporary records and can adjudicate the event
  2. If two agree, a majority can reconstruct the single event that occurred
  3. Records and memory can both be shaped by power, media selection, and loss
  4. The earliest surviving record is closest to the event, and silence indicates absence
Answer: C

ExplanationHistorical method studies selections and losses between event, record, and public memory.

H055. Aya publishes the headline first and verifies later. What is the largest institutional risk?7 marks

  1. Readers may mistake the revised headline for a separate new incident
  2. Campus units may act on the error before correction can reverse its effects
  3. Other papers may repeat the headline without preserving its original layout
  4. The author may spend class time explaining the correction, reducing regular instruction
Answer: B

ExplanationA later correction cannot automatically reverse evacuations, accusations, or resource decisions already made.

H066. The Hakurei donation box repeatedly leaves campus. Which research question is most analytical?7 marks

  1. Whether red containers leave their assigned locations more often than others
  2. Whether the last person holding the box should count as its lawful owner
  3. Whether the box initiates movement or is carried by the last person touching it
  4. How shrine and university rules of ownership, purpose, and location conflict
Answer: D

ExplanationIt locates the recurring event in competing institutional claims instead of ending with personification.
